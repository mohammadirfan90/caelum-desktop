# CR-019: Clinical records for M8: encounters, check-in, PVT, protocols, medication log, attachments

- **Status:** approved 2026-10-10 by engineering under the product owner's standing delegation.
  No new feature: implementation decisions for F-CLN-01..08 and the encrypted attachment store
  (docs/05 §2) that the documents leave open. ⚕ Clinical content (protocols, check-in
  questions, PVT thresholds) stays DRAFT under the provisional medical lead (CR-003).
- **Affects:** `caelum-security` (attachment store, AES-256-GCM), `caelum-store` (migration V6),
  `caelum-core`, `src-tauri`, `ui`, `docs/ROLE_MATRIX.md` §2–3.

## 1. Encounters (F-CLN-05)
- Created by the CMO or flight surgeon for a crew member: SOAP fields plus a **vitals snapshot**,
  the newest values at creation copied with their source and time (stored as JSON). The values
  are never re-typed.
- Editable only by the author until signed. Signing needs re-authentication (ROLE_MATRIX §4).
  A signed encounter is immutable; a correction is a new encounter. Every create, update and
  sign is audited (clinical category).
- The astronaut reads their own signed encounters (ROLE_MATRIX §2).

## 2. Daily check-in (F-CLN-07)
- Submitted only by the astronaut, about themselves: mood (1–5), symptoms (free text list),
  sleep hours. The astronaut chooses the privacy tier for each submission.
- Readers per ROLE_MATRIX §2: Private is the astronaut only; Medical is also the CMO and flight
  surgeon; Status is also the CMO and flight surgeon (MCC level only, M10). The audit records that
  a check-in was submitted, never its content.

## 3. PVT reaction test (F-CLN-06)
- Run in the window: stimuli at random intervals between 2 and 10 s for 3 minutes (the
  randomness is the test protocol, not data). Measured reaction times go to the core, which
  computes the median and the lapses (≥ 500 ms, ⚕ DRAFT) and stores `pvt_median_rt` as a
  measured observation from the station. A run with fewer than 10 responses stores nothing.
- Scheduled checks need the mission's check schedule (signed configuration); until one exists the
  Checks screen shows "Next check" as NO DATA.

## 4. Protocol library and checklists (F-CLN-02)
- Protocols come only as a signed configuration bundle of kind `protocols` (CR-012 §2 signing),
  applied by an administrator after re-authentication. No clinical protocol is shipped with the
  software. Each protocol has an ID, version, title, the rule IDs it applies to, and ordered steps.
- `protocol_list` is public: break-glass access to protocols never requires a sign-in (SR-014).
- A checklist run is started by the astronaut or CMO, optionally linked to an alarm. Each step
  is marked done or skipped. Start, steps and completion are audited and stored in
  `checklist_run`.

## 5. Medication and medical-kit log (F-CLN-08)
- New table `medication_entry`: a dose given to a crew member, or kit use or restock (no crew
  member). Written by the CMO. Read by the astronaut (own doses), the CMO and the flight surgeon.
  ROLE_MATRIX §2 gains this row.

## 6. Encrypted attachment store (docs/05 §2, F-CLN-04)
- Files (DICOM, ECG, others) are stored outside the database as AES-256-GCM ciphertext, one file
  per attachment. Each uses a random 96-bit nonce, with the attachment ID as associated data.
  The `attachment` row holds the SHA-256 of the plaintext and the metadata. Reading checks the
  tag and the hash; any mismatch is an error, never partial data.
- Key: SHA-256 of `"CAELUM-ATTACHMENT-KEY-1"` and the 32-byte random database key. This is a
  domain-separated derivation from a uniformly random secret. It needs no new keystore entry and
  cannot drift from the database. `aes-gcm` is the stack's crypto crate (CLAUDE.md).

## 7. Crew overview (F-CLN-01)
For the CMO and flight surgeon: each active crew member with their highest open alarm and the
newest heart rate, SpO₂, breathing rate and temperature, each with its freshness (SR-004).
