# CR-022: Retention, and the remaining M8 clinical-file items

- **Status:** approved 2026-10-10 by engineering under the product owner's standing delegation.
  No new feature. Decisions for F-SYS-08 (retention), F-ACQ-06 (folder watch), F-CLN-03 (events,
  segment export), F-CLN-04 (attachment links), F-CLN-06/07 (schedule).
- **Affects:** `caelum-domain::parameters` (retention class), `caelum-store`,
  `caelum-core::station::{retention, files, clinical}`, `src-tauri`, `ui`.

## 1. Retention (F-SYS-08)
- **Classes:**
  - `Mission`: physiological, clinical, environment and derived parameters, alarms, encounters,
    attachments and the audit log. Never removed by retention.
  - `Technical`: every catalog parameter of category Technical (link and data quality, SQIs).
  - Raw waveform chunks have their own limit.
- **Limits:** from a signed `retention` bundle with `technical_days`, `waveform_days` and
  `alarm_guard_minutes`. All three are required and must be ≥ 1. Without a bundle nothing is
  removed: no default limit.
- **Guard:** nothing within `alarm_guard_minutes` of an alarm of the same crew member is removed.
- **Schedule:** retention runs once a day from the tick. Each purge that removes anything is
  audited (`retention.purge`, with counts).
- **Replication:** deletions are not replicated (V9 captures inserts and updates). A standby keeps
  data at least as long as the primary.

## 2. DICOM folder watch (F-ACQ-06)
- A folder named in a signed `import` bundle (`watch_dir`, `crew_member_id`) is scanned every
  10 s.
- Each new `*.dcm` file is imported through the same path as a manual import (it must open,
  CR-014), audited as the system, then moved to `imported/` or `refused/` beside it. Nothing is
  deleted.
- **Owner:** a file is filed to the bundle's crew member only; there is no guessing from DICOM
  patient fields.
- The C-STORE SCP stays out: it needs its own network CR (CR-014 §2).

## 3. ECG review (F-CLN-03)
- **Events:** EDF+ annotations (onset, duration, text) are returned with the recording for review.
- **Segment export:** a segment (start and end within the recording, ≤ 10 min) is written as EDF+
  with the original lead labels and units, and audited. The writer keeps 16-bit resolution over
  the segment's range.

## 4. Attachments on encounters (F-CLN-04)
An attachment can be linked to an encounter that is still a draft. Signed encounters are
immutable (F-CLN-05). The link is stored on the encounter as a list of attachment IDs and audited.

## 5. Scheduled checks (F-CLN-06, F-CLN-07)
- A signed `schedule` bundle gives the PVT and check-in times as `HH:MM` UTC per day.
- The next due check is computed from it. Without a bundle the Checks screen shows NO DATA, as
  today.
