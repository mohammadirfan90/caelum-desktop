# CR-024: Onboard ground downlink (MEDICAL channel and CFDP files)

- **Status:** approved 2026-10-10 by engineering under the product owner's standing delegation.
  No new feature: wiring of F-GND-01, F-GND-05, F-GND-06 and F-GND-14 in the station.
- **Affects:** `caelum-ground::config` (optional `ground_link`), `caelum-core::station::downlink`,
  `src-tauri` (Ground status, `config_apply_contact`), `ui` (Ground screen).

## 1. Transport
- The ground configuration gains an optional `ground_link` section:
  - `endpoint`: UDP toward ground through the vehicle;
  - `receive`: local UDP port for CFDP ACKs and NAKs;
  - `summary_window_s`: summary window, s, at least 60.
- The UDP endpoint applies until ICD-VEH OI-1 agrees the vehicle interface. Path B is unchanged.
- Without `ground_link` nothing is queued for ground.

## 2. Content (MEDICAL channel)
- **Summaries (F-GND-14):** for each active crew member and each downlinked parameter with
  readings in the window: min, mean, max and count (numeric), or the last state and count
  (enum), and the worst quality. A parameter without readings sends nothing. Simulated data is
  never sent.
- **Event packets:** for every critical or warning physiological alarm raised, a JSON file
  (`caelum-event-packet/1`) with the alarm and the readings 5 minutes before and after. It is
  sent by CFDP class 2.
- **Security:** every item is signed and persisted before it can be sent (queue integrity,
  CR-016).

## 3. Contact windows
- The signed `contact-schedule` bundle is applied with `config_apply_contact` (administrator,
  re-authentication, audited).
- Outside every window nothing leaves, and CFDP transfers are suspended. They resume at the next
  window.
- Without a schedule there is no contact: everything waits. Nothing is ever sent blind.

## 4. Failure
A ground link that cannot open, or a failed round, raises `technical.ground_link` (CR-011 table).
Onboard alarms and path B never depend on the ground downlink (SR-015).
