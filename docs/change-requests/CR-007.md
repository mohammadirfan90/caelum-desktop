# CR-007: Sign-in policy and user provisioning

- **Status:** approved 2026-10-10 by engineering under the product owner's standing delegation
  ("analyze and choose the best option"). Not ⚕. No new feature: these are the policies that
  F-SYS-01 (role-based login) needs, and the documents did not yet state them.
- **Affects:** `caelum-security`, the M3 login screen; F-SYS-01, R-SEC-02, `docs/ROLE_MATRIX.md`
  §3–4.

## 1. Gap
`docs/ROLE_MATRIX.md` requires sign-in with a PIN onboard and a password on the ground, and says
`auth_login` is rate-limited. It does not fix the secret rules, the rate limit, or how the first
accounts are created.

## 2. Secrets and sign-in names
- Astronaut and CMO accounts (onboard crew): **PIN of at least 6 digits**, digits only; the
  account must belong to a crew member (their data scope).
- All other roles: **password of at least 12 characters**; never linked to a crew member.
- Every secret at most 256 bytes; stored only as an argon2id hash (CR-006 §2).
- Sign-in names: 1–64 characters `a-z 0-9 . _ -`, not case-sensitive, unique.
- Why: a 6-digit PIN with throttling (§3) and the device-bound database key is quick enough for a
  crew member in gloves or under stress; ground accounts have no such constraint.

## 3. Throttling and refusals
- After **5 consecutive failures** for one sign-in name, attempts are refused for **30 s**,
  doubling with each further failure up to **15 minutes**. A success clears the count.
  Re-authentication failures count the same way.
- Unknown names cost the same work as real ones and give the same message, so names cannot be
  probed.
- Disabled accounts are reported as such only after a correct secret.
- Device gateway accounts (machine identities) cannot use the interactive sign-in.
- **Never throttled:** break-glass (SR-014) and alarms, which do not depend on sign-in.
- Every refusal is audited (`auth.login_failed`, with the reason, never the secret).
- A locked session can call only the session commands (`auth_me`, `auth_reauth` to unlock,
  `auth_logout`); added to `docs/ROLE_MATRIX.md` §4 as a clarification.

## 4. Provisioning (implemented with the login screen in M3)
- **First administrator:** on a new installation (empty `user` table) the M3 login screen offers
  one step, "create the administrator account", and nothing else. It is audited (`user.create`,
  actor `system`) and disappears once any account exists.
- **Further accounts:** created by an administrator after re-authentication (`docs/ROLE_MATRIX.md`
  §2, "Users and roles"); each user then signs in with the initial secret.
- **Crew roster:** crew members come from signed configuration (F-SYS-07); M1 provides the
  library call (`caelum_security::users::create_user`) that both paths use.
