# CR-013: Signal processing and the sensor kit (M7)

- **Status:** approved 2026-10-10 by engineering under the product owner's standing delegation.
  No new feature: algorithm choices, draft constants and the sensor-kit ICD for F-SIG-01..07,
  F-ACQ-03, F-ACQ-05 and the baseline estimation scheduled by CR-012 §6. ⚕ Every clinical
  constant is DRAFT under the provisional medical lead (CR-003) until a licensed clinician
  countersigns.
- **Affects:** `caelum-signal`, `caelum-devices::{ghs, sensor_kit}`, `docs/icd/ICD-SENSOR-KIT.md`.

## 1. Principles
Every function is pure and deterministic, with no clock, randomness or global state. When an
output cannot be computed from the data (too short, too noisy, missing calibration or context),
the result says so and carries no value (CLAUDE.md rule 2). Every derived output carries an
algorithm identifier and version, stored as the observation's `algorithm_version`.

## 2. Filters
Butterworth second-order sections (bilinear transform), cascades, causal streaming and
zero-phase forward-backward filtering are implemented in the crate. The alarm path therefore has
no third-party numeric dependency.

## 3. Signal quality indices (F-SIG-02, SR-005)
An SQI measures contamination and sensor faults, not the presence of physiology. A clean
isoelectric line during asystole is a good signal with no beats, so the absence-of-event rule
must fire. Broadband noise, a flat line from a disconnected electrode, clipping or motion are
bad signals: the rules pause and a technical alarm is raised. Each index is the minimum of its
component scores in [0, 1]; components below 0.8 are reported as findings. All thresholds are
⚕ DRAFT engineering values.

## 4. QRS detection (F-SIG-01)
- Pan–Tompkins (1985), causal and streaming: `qrs-pan-tompkins/1`.
- CAELUM addition for safety: a peak whose 5–15 Hz amplitude is below 0.04 mV is not a QRS
  complex (`AMPLITUDE_FLOOR_MV`, ⚕ DRAFT), so baseline noise cannot mask an asystole.
- Accepted sample rates are 100–2000 Hz.
- `asystole.critical` takes its beat events from this detector, gated by `sqi_ecg ≥ 0.7`.

## 5. Heart rate and HRV (F-SIG-01)
- Heart rate is the median RR interval over 10 s (at least 3 intervals).
- NN filtering is Malik-style: intervals outside 250–3000 ms or more than 20 % from the local
  median are excluded.
- RMSSD and SDNN need at least 60 s; LF/HF (Lomb–Scargle, Task Force 1996 bands) needs at
  least 120 s (⚕ DRAFT).

## 6. Respiration (F-SIG-03)
- RIP uses the "advanced counting" method of Schäfer and Kratky (2008); EDR uses R-amplitude
  modulation.
- Pattern labels (⚕ DRAFT): `Pause` at 10 s or more without a breath, irregular at an interval
  coefficient of variation of 0.3 or more; windows need at least 20 s.

## 7. SpO₂ and pulse rate from PPG (F-SIG-04)
- SpO₂ uses the ratio of ratios with the device's own calibration curve. There is no generic
  default curve: without a calibration, no SpO₂.
- Pulse rate comes from the infrared autocorrelation.
- Motion rejection (⚕ DRAFT) rejects a window when any of these holds:
  - accelerometer motion above 0.1 g;
  - PPG SQI poor or unusable;
  - red/infrared correlation below 0.8;
  - coefficient of variation of R above 0.1 across 2 s sub-windows.

## 8. Activity and sleep/wake (F-SIG-07)
- Microgravity-safe: no posture and no 1 g is assumed. Each accelerometer axis is high-pass
  filtered at 0.25 Hz.
- Activity level is motion over a 0.5 g full scale; "at rest" is mean motion of 0.15 g or less.
- Sleep/wake uses 60 s epochs. An epoch is quiet below 0.01 g, and sleep needs 9 of the
  preceding 10 epochs quiet.
- ⚕ DRAFT: validated on Earth actigraphy only.

## 9. Core temperature (F-SIG-05)
- An extended Kalman filter observing heart rate (Buller et al. 2013) and, optionally, heat flux
  with a per-person calibrated tissue resistance: `core-temp-ekf-hr-hf/1`.
- ⚕ DRAFT: the published coefficients come from terrestrial, mostly young male, exercising
  cohorts. Their validity in microgravity needs the medical lead's review. The estimate is
  `core_temp` only and never replaces a thermometer reading (`body_temp_measured`).

## 10. Pulse arrival time and BP trend (F-SIG-06)
- PAT is measured from the R peak to the steepest PPG upstroke. The window value is the median
  of at least 5 beats.
- The BP trend is `SBP = a + b / PAT`, fitted to cuff readings. It needs:
  - at least 2 cuff points spanning 10 ms or more of PAT;
  - `b > 0`;
  - no point older than 24 h;
  - PAT within the calibrated range ±20 %.
- It is labelled a trend, and there is no population default slope (⚕ DRAFT).

## 11. Personal baselines (F-ALM-08, CR-012 §6)
- Median and unscaled MAD over a window, with a minimum count and method
  `baseline-median-mad/1`. The engine applies 1.4826 for the robust z-score.
- "At rest" baselines use only samples whose activity context is rest. Unknown context is
  excluded, never assumed (SR-018).
- Too few samples, or a zero MAD, gives no baseline.
- Windows and minimum counts per parameter are ⚕ DRAFT.

## 12. Accuracy targets and reference records (M7 gate)
- QRS sensitivity and PPV of at least 99 % on clean synthetic ECG at 40–180 bpm, and at least
  95 % with noise, baseline wander and mains interference.
- On MIT-BIH excerpts (records 100, 105, 119, 232), QRS accuracy is checked against the
  reference annotations.
- `asystole.critical` must fire on the true asystole alarms of the PhysioNet/CinC Challenge
  2015 excerpts and must not fire on its false ones, through the real alarm engine and the
  bundled ruleset.
- Fixtures are small, unmodified excerpts under ODC-By 1.0, with licence and attribution in
  `crates/caelum-signal/tests/fixtures/`.

## 13. Sensor kit and GHS (F-ACQ-03, F-ACQ-05)
- The CAELUM sensor-kit protocol is `docs/icd/ICD-SENSOR-KIT.md`, DRAFT v1: framed, versioned,
  sequence-numbered and CRC-checked. The decoder implements exactly that ICD. v1 is read only,
  so any future write needs a write-exception entry.
- The GHS decoder (IEEE 11073-10206 ACOM) reports unknown observation types as unmapped, never
  coerced. Quality comes from the Measurement Status; without one it is degraded.
