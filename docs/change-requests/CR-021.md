# CR-021: Sensor kit and signal algorithms in the station (M7 integration)

- **Status:** approved 2026-10-10 by engineering under the product owner's standing delegation.
  No new feature. Integration decisions for F-ACQ-05, F-SIG-03, F-SIG-04, F-SIG-05 and F-SIG-07,
  one catalog value, and the scope of WX-001.
- **Affects:** `caelum-devices::ble` (sensor-kit scan, Data notifications, Info read),
  `caelum-core::acquisition` (kit frames), `caelum-core::station::kit` (signal hub),
  `caelum-domain::parameters` (`sleep_stage`), `docs/write-exceptions.md` (WX-001),
  `docs/icd/ICD-SENSOR-KIT.md` (open items).

## 1. Intake
- Sensor-kit devices advertise the kit service (ICD §3). They are scanned with the health
  services, paired with the adapter `ble-sensor-kit` and connected by the device runtime.
- Data notifications are decoded per frame (`sensor_kit::decode_frame`):
  - duplicates are dropped and sequence gaps are counted (F-ACQ-10);
  - catalog readings (skin temperatures, EDA, battery) go through normal ingest (SR-017
    included);
  - the ECG goes through the existing ECG path (QRS, SQI, heart rate, asystole);
  - the other waveforms go to the signal hub.
- The Info characteristic is read once after connecting (a read). Its calibration records are
  kept for provenance and never applied again (ICD §6).

## 2. Signal hub (per device)
- A buffer holds one contiguous stretch only. A gap over 0.5 s, a rate change or an unusable
  frame restarts it, so no window spans missing data.

| Source | Window | Outputs | Algorithm |
|---|---|---|---|
| RIP (thoracic, else abdominal) | 30 s | `respiration_rate`, `sqi_resp` | `resp-count-adv/1` |
| ECG R amplitudes, only for a device without RIP | 30 s | `respiration_rate` | `resp-edr-ramp/1` |
| PPG red + infrared | 8 s | `pulse_rate`, `sqi_ppg` | `pr-ppg-acf/1` |
| Accelerometer | 60 s epoch | `activity_level`; `sleep_stage` (`wake` or `sleep`) after ten epochs | `activity-motion/1`, `sleep-wake-actigraphy-hr/1` |

- The motion context of RIP and PPG windows is the last 10 s of accelerometer data, when there
  is any.
- An unusable RIP or PPG frame stores `sqi_resp` or `sqi_ppg` = 0 with quality `unusable`
  (`sqi-device-status/1`), as for the ECG (SR-005).

## 3. Not computed, and why
- **SpO₂ from kit PPG (F-SIG-04):** the ratio-of-ratios needs the sensor's own calibration
  curve. ICD-SENSOR-KIT v1 carries none, and no generic curve is substituted (CLAUDE.md rule 2).
  No SpO₂ is produced from kit PPG until the ICD defines it (ICD OI-3). Pulse rate does not
  need it.
- **PAT and BP trend (F-SIG-06, S):** PAT needs the PPG polarity as a property of the sensor
  (`caelum-signal::pat`), and ICD v1 does not state it (ICD OI-4). BP trend also needs cuff
  calibration points. Not run until OI-4 is closed.
- **Heat-flux observation in core temperature:** it needs a per-person tissue resistance
  calibrated against a measured core temperature. It is not used until such a calibration
  exists. The estimate uses heart rate only.

## 4. Core temperature (F-SIG-05)
- **Start:** only from a measured `body_temp_measured` at a core-equivalent site (CR-003 §3 site
  classes) with good or degraded quality. A peripheral site or heart rate alone starts nothing.
- **Step:** once a minute, with the mean heart rate of that minute.
- **Stop:** without heart rate for 10 min the estimate stops until the next measurement.
- ⚕ DRAFT start variance: 0.09 °C², the ±0.3 °C maximum permissible laboratory error of ISO
  80601-2-56 taken as one standard deviation. Pending the provisional medical lead.

## 5. Catalog
- `sleep_stage` gains the value `sleep` (unstaged). Actigraphy decides sleep or wake, not a
  stage. Writing `light` or `deep` for it would be invented data.
- No rule uses `sleep_stage`.

## 6. Write exception
WX-001 (CCCD enable, category a) now names:
- the GHS Live Health Observations characteristic 0x2B8B, enabled since M7 and missing from the
  register;
- the sensor-kit Data characteristic `cd8c0002-…` (ICD §3).

No other write is added: the kit has no writable characteristic (ICD §3).
