# CR-010: Escalation audiences, sound and silence semantics

- **Status:** approved 2026-10-10 by engineering under the product owner's standing delegation;
  ⚕ items are DRAFT under the provisional medical lead (CR-003). No new feature: these are the
  semantics F-ALM-04/05 and docs/06 §2–3 need and do not state.
- **Affects:** `caelum-alarms` (`escalation`, ruleset `[escalation]`), M4 delivery; F-ALM-04,
  F-ALM-05, SR-007, SR-008, SR-015.

## 1. Escalation plans in the signed ruleset
The docs/06 §3 plans (critical: astronaut 0, CMO 0, sound 15, crew 30, ground 60; warning:
astronaut 0, CMO 0, crew 60; observe: astronaut 0, reminder 3600, CMO 86400) are clinical
configuration, so they live in `rules/ruleset.toml` (`[escalation]`), signed with the rules. A
test requires them to equal docs/06 exactly. Validation requires time order, the astronaut at 0
s, and critical plans with CMO, sound, crew and ground stages.

## 2. ⚕ Audiences
| Audience | Meaning |
|---|---|
| astronaut | The affected astronaut: the alarm with its priority tone on their station and devices |
| cmo | The crew medical officer, the same way |
| sound | Every crew station sounds the priority pattern; the wristband Immediate Alert is sent in addition, never instead (SR-015) |
| crew | The whole crew: the alarm (and for critical the emergency view, F-ALM-07) on every crew station, which also sounds |
| ground | The OPS escalation record on path B, with only the docs/06 §3a fields (a type that cannot hold more) |
| reminder | The affected astronaut again |

## 3. ⚕ Behaviour
- `CONFIRMING` spot alarms (SR-019) escalate exactly like full alarms: they are shown as alarms,
  and escalating them early is the conservative choice.
- Acknowledgement stops escalation and sound; the visual alarm stays until it ends.
- Silence is clamped to SR-008 (critical 120 s, warning 600 s), stops only the sound, never the
  escalation timers, and re-sounds when it expires. Observe alarms have nothing to silence.
- Restart (SR-007): each delivered stage is persisted as the alarm's escalation stage; after a
  restart the sound resumes and stages that fell due meanwhile are delivered at once.
- Technical alarms (advisory) have no escalation plan; they are shown on every crew station with
  the technical tone (M4 delivery).
