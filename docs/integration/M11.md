## Wiring plan

1. **Event replication:** every station write that must survive a takeover becomes one log
   entry, appended by the primary through the node driver:
   - observations;
   - alarm creation and state changes;
   - escalation stages;
   - acknowledgements and silences;
   - audit entries;
   - roster, account and device changes.

   The standby applies the entries to its own encrypted database in order. Alarm escalation
   resumes from the replicated stage on promotion (SR-007 restore path).
2. **Promotion:** on `Action::Promote` the new primary restores open alarms and their stages
   (the existing restart path), tells devices to reconnect (device runtime), and starts the
   path B publisher and the MDS service.
3. **Instance role:** the status footer shows primary / standby / fenced from the node state,
   instead of NO DATA.
4. **Provisioning:** the crew-LAN CA, station certificates and the registry come from a signed
   configuration bundle (kind `cluster`), applied by an administrator. Station certificates and
   keys live in the OS keystore.
5. **Secondary and kiosk stations:** a secondary station's shell connects to `/v1/sessions` and
   `/v1/alarms/stream`. A kiosk subscribes to `/v1/emergency/stream`; in-app kiosk mode (`--kiosk`)
   already exists (F-SYS-13).
6. **Third-party-risk rows:** axum, hyper, hyper-util, tokio-rustls, tokio-tungstenite (CR-017 §2).
